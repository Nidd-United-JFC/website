Club Shop

Club Shop

Our kit supplier has their own online shop that you can order direct from.

You only need to purchase a new kit if you are new to the club, your team has a new match kit sponsor, or if last season’s kit no longer fits.

If you have any questions about purchasing your kit from the supplier, ask your coach.

Sizing Information

To ensure the best fit, please consult our sizing chart, which you can view here: Sizing Guide.

Delivery

Orders placed with the supplier will be delivered direct to you. Delivery charges are calculated at checkout. Bulk orders of new match kit tops will be delivered to your coach for distribution.

Before You Order

You'll need to know the following:

  • - Kit Size: You will need to know the size of the kit you want to order - see the sizing guide here

  • - Squad Number: You'll need to know your allocated squad number to complete your purchase. If you're unsure of your squad number, please confirm with your coach before ordering. The Squad number needs to be added to the text box labeled 'INITIALS'. For Match Kit: Do NOT put initials in the box, just your squad number.

  • - Sponsored Kits: If your team has an existing match kit sponsor from last season, you can order your match kit tops via the club shop as normal - just select your team's sponsor when ordering. Branding will be added to the front of your shirt.

  • - New Sponsors: If your team has a new match kit sponsor, the initial purchase of match kit tops will be made in bulk by the club - your coach will ask you for your top size, so you don't need to order this yourself. You will still need to purchase any shorts or socks directly from the supplier. After this initial bulk order, if you need any additional tops (e.g. a replacement or a new player joining), these must be ordered directly from the supplier, making sure to select the correct sponsor.

For the attention of coaches: if your team has a new match kit sponsor this season, please see our Coach's Guidance for Ordering New Sponsored Match Kit for the process to follow.

FAQs

Q: We already have kit from last year, do we have to buy different kit?

A: No, you do not need to buy new kit if your current kit still fits. If you had a match kit from last season, but you are no longer being sponsored this year, you do not need to purchase a new one. Any new purchase of match kit should not include the old sponsors logo

Q: Our team has a sponsored match kit, how do we order?

A: If you have a new Match Kit sponsor, this should not be purchased through the club shop, but through your coach - your coach will collect your match kit shirt size, and a bulk order will be made by the club using sponsor funds so that it costs the players nothing. If you have an existing Match Kit sponsor, this should be purchased through the club shop directly from the supplier, making sure to choose the correct sponsor at checkout. Any additional sponsored Match Kit tops throughout the season should be purchased directly from the supplier through the club shop.

Q: Can I purchase individual items?

A: You can buy any individual items from the shop.

Q: How do I know which size to order?

A: Please refer to our sizing chart here to find the best fit for you.

Q: How do I provide a squad number when ordering?

A: You’ll need to provide your allocated squad number and put it in the box labeled 'INITIALS' on the supplier's order form. If you're unsure of your squad number, please confirm with your coach.
