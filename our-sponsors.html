Our Sponsors

We are incredibly grateful for each of our sponsors, who by investing in the club, have enabled us to continue to provide quality football opportunities for young people across the Nidd Valley.

Concrete4U

Match Kit Sponsor - U18

Concrete4U is a family-owned supplier of concrete and aggregates serving North Yorkshire, including Harrogate, Hambleton, Richmondshire and York. They offer everything from ready-mixed concrete to liquid flowing screeds and aggregates, with flexible delivery including evenings, weekends and night work.

Visit their website

ETL Fluid Experts

Match Kit Sponsor - U12 Reds

ETL Fluid Experts Ltd is a privately owned company based in Knaresborough specialising in oil filtration, coolant handling and high-performance metalworking fluids. They operate internationally, serving the industrial, metalworking, marine and gas engine sectors while working to minimise environmental impact.

Visit their website

Great North Auto Services

Match Kit Sponsor - U14

Great North Auto Services runs two modern workshops in Harrogate and Knaresborough, offering a genuine alternative to main dealer servicing and repair for cars, vans and fleet operators. Their qualified technicians provide servicing, MOT testing, repairs and specialist diagnostics.

Visit their website

Cleveland Steel & Tubes

Match Kit Sponsor - U16 West

Cleveland Steel & Tubes is a steel stockholder with over 45 years' experience supplying seamless and welded tube for the construction, process, energy, mechanical and fluid power industries, part of the multinational Bianco Group, holding around 70,000 tonnes of inventory at their North Yorkshire site.

Visit their website

Retro Games

Team Jacket Sponsor - U12 Reds

Retro Games Ltd specialises in recreating classic computing and gaming experiences through officially licensed hardware products, including reimagined versions of the Commodore 64, Amiga, ZX Spectrum and Atari computers, alongside complementary accessories for enthusiasts and collectors.

Visit their website
